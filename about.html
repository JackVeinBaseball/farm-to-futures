About

From the farm system to the front office

Farm to Futures covers the full pipeline — draft to development to the 26-man — written the way a scouting or player-development report gets written: specific, sourced, and willing to take a real position.

I'm [Your Name] — I write about roster construction, prospect development, and farm systems, drawing on time spent working inside a professional baseball operations department. This site is where I put that thinking in public: opinions on trades and extensions, deep dives on individual prospects, and tiered breakdowns of full farm systems.

Every post carries a depth meter instead of a read-time estimate — borrowed from the 20-80 scouting scale. A 40 means a quick, accessible take. An 80 means you should expect stat tables and mechanics talk.
