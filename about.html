About

From the farm system to the front office

Farm to Futures covers the full pipeline — draft to development to the 26-man: roster strategy, prospect reports, farm system tiers, and field notes from the inside. Every post carries a depth meter instead of a read-time estimate, borrowed from the 20-80 scouting scale — 40 means a quick take, 80 means expect stat tables and mechanics talk.

About Me

Jack Vein

I recently wrapped up an internship in Baseball Operations with the New York Mets, where I built 40-man roster planning models — factoring in option status, waiver rules, Rule 5 eligibility, service time, and organizational depth — and projected multiyear arbitration values to help inform payroll and roster decisions. I also supported Draft and Trade Deadline work, evaluating amateur and professional talent and preparing scouting reports that blend Hawk-Eye data, video, and internal scouting notes.

Before the Mets, I worked in Baseball Operations at THE·TEAM (Wasserman), researching salary arbitration comps for a roster of pitchers and hitters, building player evaluation decks for college clients, and developing an amateur baseball database using Synergy and R to sharpen prospect identification. I spent a prior summer with MLB's Auctions and Authentication group, and I founded and still run Jack's Trading Cards, a baseball card and memorabilia business built on a self-designed Excel model that projects big-league success using stats like 90th-percentile exit velocity and xwOBA — the same model I've used to identify breakout talent early, including Corbin Carroll and Pete Crow-Armstrong.

I graduated from Washington University's Olin Business School with a degree in Finance and a minor in the Business of Sports, where I was also Treasurer of the Business of Sports Society. I was recognized as an eligible candidate for the MLB Billy Bean Fellowship. This fall, I'll be starting Columbia's MS in Sports Management program, where I plan to work with the team and the SABRlions group to keep sharpening my analytical and evaluation skills.
